## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

```
Electronic Helper puts the calculators and reference tables you reach for most into one app. Work out a resistor, read a colour code, check a pinout, and get back to building.

It works offline, shows no adverts and asks for no permissions.

CALCULATORS
Results update as you type, and every field has a unit picker, so you can enter 4.7 kΩ or 100 nF directly.

• Ohm's law and power, from any two values
• Voltage divider, with a load
• Resistors and capacitors in series and parallel
• LED series resistor, with the nearest standard value and wattage
• RC time constant and RC filter cut-off
• Reactance and LC resonance
• 555 timer: astable and monostable
• RMS and peak, frequency and wavelength
• Op-amp gain, decibels and dBm
• Battery life
• LM317 output and linear regulator heat
• Heat sink thermal resistance
• Wire voltage drop and PCB trace width
• Number base converter and ADC resolution

COMPONENT CODES
• Resistor colour code for 4, 5 and 6 bands. Pick the colours, or type a value to see its bands.
• SMD resistor codes, including EIA-96
• Capacitor codes
• Nearest standard value from E6 to E96

LOGIC GATES
Pick a gate, flip its inputs and watch the symbol, output and truth table change. Covers AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR and XNOR.

REFERENCE
• Transistor ratings and pin orders
• Pinouts for the 555, common op-amps, regulators and 7400-series logic
• LED forward voltages
• Switch types
• USB pinouts and power limits
• Coaxial connectors and cables
• Wire gauge table
• Battery voltages and capacities
• Arduino board specifications and Arduino UNO pin functions
• Raspberry Pi 40-pin GPIO header
• Mains plug types, and the voltage, frequency and plugs used in 129 countries and territories
• A formula sheet and SI prefixes
• A reading list of free textbooks and courses

MADE TO BE USED
• Search every tool, or star your favourites to keep them at the top
• Light and dark themes
• Tap a result to copy it, or share the whole calculation
• Works on phones, tablets and foldables

Results are a guide. Check the datasheet before you build anything that matters.

Electronic Helper is open source. Arduino and Raspberry Pi are trademarks of their respective owners; this app is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
```
