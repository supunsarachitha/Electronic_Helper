Privacy policy

Applies to the Electronic Helper Android app (lk.stechbuzz.electronic), version 2.0.0 and later. Last updated 10 October 2026.

Electronic Helper does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no adverts, no analytics and no accounts, and it asks for no permissions.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code from any third party.

The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the network by itself.

What stays on your device

The list of tools you mark as favourites is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is used only to show those tools at the top of the home screen. Nothing you type into a calculator is saved.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include this list in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so that it comes back when you reinstall the app. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings, and the developer has no access to it. Uninstalling the app deletes the list from your device.

Things you choose to send

  • Sharing a result. If you tap Share, the result is handed to the app you pick, such as a messaging app. What happens to it then is up to that app.
  • Opening a link. The reading list, the Rate button and this policy open in your web browser or the Play Store. Those sites and services have their own privacy policies.
  • Sending feedback. If you tap Send feedback, your e-mail app opens with a message addressed to the developer. If you send it, the developer receives your e-mail address and whatever you write, and uses them only to read and answer your message.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to stechbuzz@gmail.com. The app's source code is public, so you can check these statements for yourself on GitHub.
