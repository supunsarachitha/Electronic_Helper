Everything in one place

28 calculators that update as you type, two interactive tools and 18 reference pages. Search for any of them, or star the ones you use most to keep them at the top.

Circuit basics

  • Ohm's law and power, from any two values
  • Voltage divider, with a load
  • Resistors and capacitors in series and parallel
  • LED series resistor, with standard value and wattage
  • Capacitor charge and energy

Components

  • Resistor colour code for 4, 5 and 6 bands
  • SMD resistor and capacitor code decoders
  • Nearest E6 to E96 standard value
  • LED voltages, transistors and IC pinouts
  • Switch types

Signals and timing

  • RC time constant and RC filters
  • Reactance and LC resonance
  • 555 timer, astable and monostable
  • RMS and peak, frequency and wavelength
  • Op-amp gain, decibels and dBm

Power and wiring

  • Battery life
  • LM317 and linear regulator heat
  • Heat sink sizing
  • Wire voltage drop and PCB trace width
  • Wire gauge and battery tables

Digital and boards

  • Logic gate simulator with truth tables
  • Number base converter, up to 64 bits
  • ADC resolution
  • Arduino board specifications and UNO pins
  • Raspberry Pi GPIO header

Connectors and mains

  • USB pinouts and power limits
  • Coaxial connectors and cables
  • Mains plug types A to O
  • Voltage, frequency and plugs for 129 countries and territories
  • A reading list of free textbooks and courses
